How I Work

Context Before Assumptions

I begin by understanding the business situation in context—its objectives, constraints, stakeholders, and operating environment—before defining solutions. This ensures decisions are grounded in real business needs rather than symptoms.

From Ambiguity to Structure

I bring structure to ambiguity by analysing needs, clarifying priorities, and framing options so decision-makers can act with confidence. The goal is shared understanding, not documentation for its own sake.

Early Stakeholder Alignment

I focus on aligning stakeholders early—balancing perspectives, managing expectations, and ensuring decisions reflect both business value and operational reality.

Outcomes Over Activity

I measure success through outcomes such as risk reduction, improved efficiency, and sustainable change. Analysis only matters when it leads to informed decisions and durable execution.
